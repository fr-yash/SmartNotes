# 📋 User Suspension & Appeal Guide

## What Happens When Your Account is Suspended?

When an admin suspends your account:
- ❌ You **cannot login** to your account
- ❌ You **cannot access** your notes
- ❌ You **cannot use** AI features
- ✅ You **can submit** an appeal to request reactivation

## How to Request Account Reactivation

### Step 1: Try to Login
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll see the message: **"Your account has been suspended. Please contact support."**

### Step 2: You'll Be Redirected to Appeal Page
The system automatically redirects you to the suspension appeal page where you can:
- View your account status
- Submit an appeal
- Check previous appeal requests

### Step 3: Submit Your Appeal
1. Click on the appeal form
2. Write a detailed reason explaining why your account should be reactivated
   - **Minimum 10 characters required**
   - Be honest and specific
   - Explain what happened and why it won't happen again
3. Click **"Submit Appeal"**
4. You'll see: **"Appeal submitted successfully! An admin will review it soon."**

### Step 4: Wait for Admin Response
- Your appeal status will show as **"Pending"**
- An admin will review your appeal
- You'll receive a response with either:
  - ✅ **Approved**: Your account is reactivated! You can login again.
  - ❌ **Rejected**: Your account remains suspended. The admin will explain why.

### Step 5: Check Your Appeal History
On the suspension appeal page, you can see:
- All your previous appeals
- The status of each appeal (Pending, Approved, Rejected)
- Admin's response to each appeal
- When each appeal was submitted and resolved

## Appeal Tips

### ✅ DO:
- Be honest and sincere in your appeal
- Explain the situation clearly
- Show understanding of why you were suspended
- Demonstrate commitment to following rules
- Be respectful in your tone

### ❌ DON'T:
- Submit multiple appeals for the same issue
- Be rude or disrespectful
- Make excuses without taking responsibility
- Submit vague or very short reasons
- Try to bypass the system

## What Happens After Approval?

If your appeal is **approved**:
1. Your account is automatically reactivated
2. You can login with your email and password
3. All your notes and data are restored
4. You can use all features normally

## What if My Appeal is Rejected?

If your appeal is **rejected**:
1. The admin will provide a reason
2. Your account remains suspended
3. You can submit another appeal after some time
4. Contact support if you have questions about the rejection

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does it take to get a response?**
A: Admins typically review appeals within 24-48 hours.

**Q: Can I submit multiple appeals?**
A: You can only have one pending appeal at a time. Wait for a response before submitting another.

**Q: What if I disagree with the rejection?**
A: You can submit another appeal with additional information or context.

**Q: Will my data be deleted if I'm suspended?**
A: No, your notes and data are preserved. They'll be accessible once your account is reactivated.

**Q: Can I contact support directly?**
A: The appeal system is the official way to request reactivation. Submit a detailed appeal for best results.

**Q: How many times can I appeal?**
A: You can submit multiple appeals, but only one can be pending at a time.

## Need Help?

If you have questions about your suspension or appeal:
1. Check the admin's response in your appeal history
2. Submit a new appeal with your questions
3. Be specific about what you need help with

---

**Remember**: The appeal system is designed to give you a fair chance to explain your situation and request account reactivation. Be honest, respectful, and detailed in your appeal for the best outcome.
